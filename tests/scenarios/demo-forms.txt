# about:demo's forms: a click on a label checks its checkbox, one on the
# other radio button's label moves the group's check to it, a click on a
# box itself does too, Space on the focused checkbox toggles it, a disabled
# fieldset's checkbox stays as it is, and the reset button puts the form
# back. The boxes have their native looks, checked or not and disabled
# (the screenshot shows them). Below them the
# unstyled controls are Chrome's sizes (those that don't follow the font:
# the text field's and the text area's are in the screenshot), a tall
# button's text is in its middle, and a list box is 3 rows high (the
# select and the list box are in the screenshot too). The list box to pick
# from takes a click and Shift+ArrowDown, the select beside it opens its
# list on a click, ArrowDown and Enter pick the next option
# (`list-box.txt` has more). The number field steps by 5 with ArrowUp,
# the slider by 10 with ArrowRight, and Escape empties the search field
# (the spin button's press is in `shell`'s tests).
