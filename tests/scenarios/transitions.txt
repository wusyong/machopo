# about:demo's transition: "Move the bar" makes the bar wider and green
# over 600 ms (two frames after the click it is partway: the page's first
# transition doesn't jump to its end), and each property's transitionend
# reaches the note; again, it goes back, partway too. The screenshots
# show the bar narrow and blue, then wide and green; whether it moves
# smoothly is for a person to look at.
window size=800x1000
navigate about:demo
wait-loaded
scroll-to #tr-bar
screenshot bar
click #tr-move
wait-text #tr-frame "Partway two frames in."
wait-text #tr-note "Moved: background-color and width ended."
screenshot bar-moved
click #tr-move
wait-text #tr-frame "Partway two frames in."
wait-text #tr-note "Back: background-color and width ended."
