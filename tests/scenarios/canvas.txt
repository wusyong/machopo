# about:demo's canvas: its 2D context's background and squares are
# painted; clicking the canvas adds a square; "Add one later" adds one
# from an idle callback a second later, which changes no DOM and, unlike a
# timer's or an event's, asks for no frame: nothing but the canvas asks for
# the one that shows it. "Read a pixel" reads the first square back with
# getImageData. The paths' canvas is painted, and "New size" gives it a
# new width, which clears it and makes it narrower, drawn again. The text's
# canvas is painted (filled and stroked text, and a box from measureText),
# and "Bigger text" draws it again larger. The styles' canvas is painted
# (gradients, a pattern, a shadow, a canvas drawn on it, Path2Ds
# composited "multiply"), and clicking its middle finds the circle with
# isPointInPath and turns its box's colors around with putImageData.
# "Copy as image" shows the styles canvas, as toBlob's PNG through a blob
# URL, in an image below it.
window size=800x600
navigate about:demo
wait-loaded
scroll-to #canvas-demo
capture squares
screenshot squares
click #canvas-demo
expect-differs squares #canvas-demo
screenshot added
click #canvas-later
capture clicked
expect-differs clicked #canvas-demo
screenshot later
click #canvas-read
wait-text #canvas-note "The first square is rgba(255, 153, 0, 1)."
scroll-to #canvas-paths
capture paths
screenshot paths
click #canvas-resize
wait-text #canvas-paths-note "180 pixels wide."
expect-differs paths #canvas-paths
screenshot resized
scroll-to #canvas-text
capture text
screenshot text
click #canvas-bigger
wait-text #canvas-text-note "Text at 18px."
scroll-to #canvas-text
expect-differs text #canvas-text
screenshot bigger
scroll-to #canvas-styles
capture styles
screenshot styles
click #canvas-styles
wait-text #canvas-styles-note "You picked the circle."
expect-differs styles #canvas-styles
screenshot picked
click #canvas-copy
wait-text #canvas-copy-note "Copied as image/png: the image below."
scroll-to #canvas-copy-img
screenshot copied
