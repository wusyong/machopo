# The form pseudo-classes follow the user: a click on a label checks its
# checkbox, and `input:checked ~ #mark` widens the mark; the reset button
# unchecks it, and the mark is narrow again. Both change the checkbox outside
# script's calls, which the renderer catches up with before it draws the next
# frame (the frames are compared, not the boxes, which a query would bring up
# to date itself).

capture unchecked
click #label
expect-differs unchecked
screenshot checked
capture checked
click #reset
expect-differs checked
