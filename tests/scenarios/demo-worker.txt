# about:demo's "A worker busy for 3 seconds": while a dedicated worker
# keeps its own thread busy, the page's main thread isn't: a click is
# answered at once, and the worker's message comes once it is done.
# Tall enough that every element clicked is in the window.
window size=800x1000
navigate about:demo
wait-loaded
click #worker
wait-text #worker-note "The worker is busy on its own thread: the page goes on."
# A second is far less than the 3 seconds the worker is busy for.
timeout 1
click #clicker
wait-text #count "Clicked 1 time (onclick)."
timeout 10
wait-text #worker-note "Done: the worker was busy for 3 seconds, the page wasn't."
screenshot after
