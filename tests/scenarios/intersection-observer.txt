# about:demo's IntersectionObserver: the note under the list says how much
# of its last box shows in it, as the observer last said (its thresholds:
# none, half and all of it). The box starts below the list's bottom; the
# wheel scrolls the list (the compositor moves it, the main thread hears
# of it) until half of the box shows, then all of it.
window size=800x600
navigate about:demo

scroll-to #io-list
wait-text #io-note "Out of view."
wheel #io-list 0 70
wait-text #io-note "In view: 50%."
wheel #io-list 0 30
wait-text #io-note "In view: 100%."
